Position space. The density |ψ|² sits on the line E = H(x0, k0) above the potential; dotted parts of that line are classically forbidden. The two ends of the axis are the same point of the ring.

Phase space. Grey lines are classical orbits and the coloured curve is the separatrix. The shading is the exact Husimi function, the quantum state smoothed over one coherent-state cell, so it always looks a little wider than the ellipse.

Ellipse. The covariance Δ(t) from the linearized equations, carried by the classical trajectory. The classical swarm shows the full nonlinear classical flow of the same initial Gaussian.

Scaled units m = λ = 1: momenta in units of √(mλ), time in units of √(m/λ), w in units of 1/√(mλ). The width σq is measured in a local angular coordinate around the packet's mean angle.

Numerics: split-step Fourier evolution on the ring (512 grid points, Δt = 0.005) of a periodized Gaussian; classical and semiclassical equations by fourth-order Runge–Kutta.
